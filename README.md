# Market Stability Architecture / SymC Economics

**Current research notice: 21 September 2026**

This repository contains the economics and market-microstructure arm of the SymC research program. The active reconstruction is on branch:

`market-chi-architecture`

The current program is native-market-first and governed by SymC General Operations Manual v0.8.3. It does **not** assume that financial markets are damped harmonic oscillators, that a universal scalar chi exists, or that chi = 1 is a universal market optimum.

## Current architecture

Uppercase **Chi / Χ** denotes the broader reconstructed market stability architecture. Lowercase **chi / χ** is reserved for a local or modal scalar only when a licensed dynamical model earns that reduction.

The preferred evidence path is:

```text
native market observables
-> qualified statistical/dynamical structure
-> modal/vector and system organization
-> local chi only where licensed
-> Function Map + Limit Map
-> diagnostic/predictive tests
```

Local asset or mode coordinates are not averaged into a market-wide scalar without an independent system-level derivation. Under GOM v0.8.3, the relationship between any admitted local chi and broader market Chi is itself tested, while perturbation/recovery is decomposed rather than treated as a single stability label.

## Scientific guardrails

Current work requires:

- subject/event/time-order leakage controls appropriate to market data;
- explicit handling of rolling-window dependence and session phase;
- contract-roll safeguards;
- native alternatives must be allowed to beat SymC representations;
- direction, move capacity, stability, forcing, liquidity, and order flow remain distinct unless evidence links them;
- rare crashes are Limit-Map cases, not the sole definition of normal market function;
- the Market Atlas may interpret Engine output but may not tune the Engine;
- diagnostic and predictive claims are separate;
- synthetic oscillator recovery qualifies a method, not the ontology of a real market.
