## Why v0.2 was required

v0.1 was written before completion of the substantial recovery-theory audit and exact \(\chi\)-collision search.

Those reviews established that:
- recovery rate, resistance, finite-time return, basin resilience, reactivity, invariability, memory, baseline migration, and repeated-shock resilience are distinct;
- slower recovery does not imply smaller basin or lower transition threshold;
- non-normal systems can transiently amplify while remaining asymptotically stable;
- repeated shocks before independence time can mimic degradation;
- moving baselines can mimic changing recovery;
- scalar thresholds, market phase transitions, repeated-shock resilience, and multiscale fragility are already prior art;
- the bounded residual is the joint qualification/discrimination architecture, not invention of \(\chi\).
