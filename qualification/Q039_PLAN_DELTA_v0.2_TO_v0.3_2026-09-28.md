2. Layer R now distinguishes standardized-coordinate Q037/Q038 lineage directions from the covector-consistent image of raw semantic D/I functionals.

5. Carry-forward is treated as a measurement convention under test. Coarse staleness/update controls and NC7 matched-update-timing null are added.
6. The predictive baseline is strengthened by fine-native activity/update structure before semantic fine features enter.
7. Factor-2 pairs are explicitly last-fast-equivalent semantic tests, not path tests.

12. `SUBTRACTS_P0D` is retired. Negative OOS penalties remain quantitative but are not interpreted as active negative information.
13. Clark-West-style adjusted nested-model contrasts are frozen as diagnostics only and cannot rescue raw OOS gain.
14. `FINE_VARIABILITY_OR_LAST_ONLY_P0D` is retired. A passing A->S with nonpassing U->S becomes `FINE_SEMANTIC_INFO_ADDS_ORDER_NOT_RESOLVED_P0D`.
15. NC1-NC8 now include latent-regime, matched-update-timing carry-forward, and generic calendar/common-mode worlds.
